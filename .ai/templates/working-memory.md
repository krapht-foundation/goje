# Working memory template

Copy this outline to `.ai/memories/working/<topic>.md` when ongoing work needs a repository handoff. Remove the handoff when the work closes; promote durable facts to the owning documents listed in `.ai/README.md`.

- **Owner and work item:** Person or team, plus issue or pull request.
- **State:** Date, active or blocked, and the current objective.
- **Evidence:** Files, observations, and checks that actually ran.
- **Open questions:** Unresolved choices and who can decide them.
- **Next action:** What to do first when resuming.
