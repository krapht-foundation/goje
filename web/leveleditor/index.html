Arrows move the cell cursor. Space or Enter applies the tool; with Move placement, choose a placement and then its target; with Add placement, choose a free floor cell for a placement of the kind and ID in the Placements panel; with Draw zone, choose one corner and then the opposite corner, using the kind, role, and heights in the Zones panel. Delete removes the selected placement. Escape cancels. Ctrl or ⌘ + Z undoes; add Shift to redo.
