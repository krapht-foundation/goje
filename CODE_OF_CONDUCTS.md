# Code of conduct

Goje welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and events where participants represent Goje.

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect. Critique work rather than the person doing it, explain disagreements constructively, and respect personal boundaries and privacy. Help new participants understand how to contribute.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language directed at others, publication of private information without consent, and retaliation against anyone who reports an incident are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the [Krapht Foundation contact](mailto:mostafa.sh.coderino@gmail.com) with `Goje conduct` in the subject. Include what happened, where and when it occurred, and any context you are comfortable sharing. Do not post a conduct report in a public issue. If the report concerns the contact or you cannot use that channel safely, [report the content or account to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people who need the information to respond. A maintainer involved in the report should not handle it. Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant. Retaliation may lead to further action.
